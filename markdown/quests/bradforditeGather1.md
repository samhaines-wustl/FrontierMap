# Finding Clarice Hampton

*Completed*

Starrider Ruby Glass, current leader of the Bradfordites, is shocked at what you reveal. All their numbers, charts, materials, are completely off for their ride to the stars. The only way to fix this is reaching out to the man who originally made these schematics, the best mathematician the Bradfordites ever knew, Clarice Hampton. In his old age, he retired to Hope's Scar, tending to a quaint melon farm. Your task, is to convince him to come back and rejoin the Bradfordites.

### Reward
 - Great favor with the Bradfordites and whatever Clarice throws in.
