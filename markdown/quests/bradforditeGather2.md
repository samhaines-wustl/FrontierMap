# Finding Bulldozer Buckner

*Completed*

Nostalgia has seeped into Clarice's mind and he's been swayed to rejoin the Bradfordites; however, he pines after some of the old guard. If they are going to truly get their steel horse to the stars, their best blacksmith needs to come back too. Bulldozer Buckner, an old dwarven woman, scorned by the false promise of the stars, has closed herself in at her smith in Softtown. Do whatever it takes, Bulldozer needs to come home.

### Reward
 - Great favor with the Bradfordites and whatever Bulldozer throws in.
