# Finding Rabbit Stockton

*Ongoing*

Bulldozer has found a way to reconcile her differences and come back to the Bradfordites, but there's someone else who must be fetched. As Bulldozer said, "He's the best fucking chemist who walks these sands". Last anyone heard, Rabbit Stockton gave up all earthly possessions and retreted to the top of Pinnacle's Spine. Whether he's at home in a monastery or walks the jagged cliffs is unknown. But, if the Bradfordites are to get their steel horse off the ground, they need a man who knows more about oil than there are stars in the sky. Rabbit must rejoin.

### Reward
 - Great favor with the Bradfordites and whatever Rabbit throws in.
