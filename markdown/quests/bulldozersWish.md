# Before I go, I want to leave my mark

*Completed*

Bulldozer Buckner is beginning to be swayed back to the Bradfordites, but wants to conquer one last challenge before leaving Softtown. She wishes to slay the local beast, the Chimeric Manticore. To take down this foe, she wants you to get her a saber-tooth tiger, the favorite prey of the Manitcore, help her convert it into a molten metal shrapnel bomb, and initiate the Chimeric Manticore with that. Good luck.

### Reward
 - Bulldozer Buckner rejoins the Bradfordites
