# This studded leather is way too heavy man

*Completed*

Madame LaRoche's tailor has crafted you some of the best garbs to blend in with the King's Rangers. There's one problem, it is too heavy. Since clothing is so important to the King's Rangers, you'll need a good substitute. The tailor proposes hunting the Chimeric Manticore and using its leather skin to craft new disguises. This armor should be much lighter and more to your liking.
