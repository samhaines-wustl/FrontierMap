# It is time to quell this unruly mob

*Ongoing*

Madame LaRoche has big plans for the Nokomont Midlands and beyond, but it must start with getting rid of the frieghtful presences of the King's Rangers. Even though she's disseminated her pearl pieces across nearby towns, their adoption has been slowed. She's asked her mayors, representatives, and friends to encourage their use, but they all come back to her saying the same thing: The King's Rangers are scaring the populace too much.

Though they keep the Red Hand of Doom at bay, their unruly nature and immature attics are too volatile for the stability need for a currency overhaul. Madame LaRoche has tasked you with infilitrating the King's Rangers and taking them out from the inside. She has a contact who's been integrated with the King's Rangers for a year now named Elio Rivera, he'll help you fit right in.

### Reward
 - 500 pearl pieces each and 2 artifacts from her treasury.
