# Reid needs those pelts and those buffalos dead

*Ongoing*

You've bargained your way into a hide and leather contract with Ariel Reid. You've promised her no less than 50 hides from the Buffalo out in the Nokomont Midlands, taking a substantial hit on the price per hide if that quota isn't met. Though it may take time, as long as Ariel's still in business, the deal will be honored. Since you're green at this, she's referred you to a local supply shop, Dunahue's Supply, where you can get some wagon and oxen on loan and buy the rations you'll need.

### Reward
 - 30gp per hide. Decreases to 15gp if 50 hide quota is met
 - 25% bonus with intact innards keg
