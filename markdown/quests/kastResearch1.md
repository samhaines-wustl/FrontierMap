# Science and study demands more crystals

*Ongoing*

After helping Deep Red with her crystal studies, she sees promise in the party. With her overwhelming grant money from Dr. Kast, Deep Red wants to hire you to trek to the Rachelford Mines and retrieve another crytal for her. Just getting two crystals this close together is sure to birth a whale of new knowledge, and Deep Red will pay you handsomely for it. She does warm you however, that the Rachelford Mines have been corralled by the Oblivion Gang, a fearsome organization she's only heard bad things about.
