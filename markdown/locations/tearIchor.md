# Deep Red's Research Site

*A shody shack surrounding a pulsating, almost-alive, crystal*

### Places
 - **Mysterious Crystal**: Sprouting from the ground, this massive pyramid was summon by the words of the Grisantian Lion. 

### People
 - **Deep Red**: Undercover gnome investigator for the Children of the Wax who's fully integrated into this lion pride. She's unsure about her discoveries, but has learned lots about these peculiar lions and their relationship with the magic crystal they worship.

*The Grisantian Lion shares its eyes and mane. Is it generous or will it collect on this debt?*
