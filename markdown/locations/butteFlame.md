# Inferno Butte

*Headquarter's for the King's Rangers*

### People
 - **Wildfire Teddy Sunshine**: The head of the King's Rangers who has mostly retreated to the background. No one dares defy him but winds are changing.

 - **Exalted Ranger Bob**: Easily the most popular Ranger, he lead the raid on the abadoned candelabra and extinguished the Red Hand of Doom (for now). He was even blessed with Pedro the Lobster as a spoil of battle. His march to greatness seems unopposed.

 - **Trapper Fleck**: Ranger Bob's boyfriend and definitely not a ranger. Don't let his oily black hair, hunched-back, and coarse voice fool you, he seems up to no good, always whispering secrets to Bob's ears only.

 - **Ranger Jordan May Morgan**: The number two of Ranger Bob, an eager dwarf looking to do anything to impress Ranger Bob.

 - **Ranger Golden Emmanuel**: An old human Ranger, all his hair either gone or bright white. He reminisces for a time when the Rangers were something more; more dignified, more honorable, more noble.
