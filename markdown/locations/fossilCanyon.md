# Fossil Canyon

*The walls of this canyon are a parade of dinosaur bones*

### Places
 - **Woman Tree**: A blossom banyan tree created by Dr. Kast with some of the juicy fruit you've ever tasted. However, pressed in its roots and bark is the undeniable corpse of a woman, and she speaks. 

### People
 - **Woman Tree/Irene Banks**: Former member of the True World, she was transformed against her will and now begs for death. Will you grant it to her. 

 - **Emmett Bailey**: Fish-hybrid gardener for the banyan tree. As a thanks to Dr. Kast for saving his life, he now tends to and protects the banyan tree.
