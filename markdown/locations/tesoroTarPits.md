# Tesoro Tar Pits

*A collection of thick toxic tar pits*

### Places
 - **Delian Tomb**: Underneath the pits, if one can get there, is an ancient tomb. Rumor has it that the Cylinder of Spite is buried here, or was it the Winchester Book?
 
### People
 - **Tentacles of the Pit**: The monster that lurks beneath. It has the body of a cow, tentacles of a squid, and a gapping maw unlike any beast. It loves this sewage.

*Rumor has it there is treasure beneath the pits, or at least there used to be*
