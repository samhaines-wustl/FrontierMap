# Starbend Hill

*It's dazzling: the stars, the people, the broken observatory*

### Places
 - **Broken Observatory**: The centerpiece of the town, a destroyed observatory with scaffolding surrounding it like a cage. There's an vacancy in the center, waiting for a large telescope to move in.
 
 - **Oilyard**: It's where they store their oil of course! They need lots for their trip to the stars

### People
 - **Starrider Ruby Glass**: Leader of the Bradfordites, she's a human who wishes she saw Captain Bradford crash down on his metal horse. She will stop at nothing to bring her people to the stars.

*How these fools are still alive and making progress is beyond me*
