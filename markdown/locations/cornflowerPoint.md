# Cornflower Point

*The main town in the Death Basin*

### Places
 - **Monday's Parasols**: For all your heat beating needs.
 
 - **The Vague Cello**: The local bar and inn, accomodating to all.

### People
 - **Dame Hattie Cotton**: An old halfling alchemist. She's knows her way around a poison like no one else, and always has an odd job if needed.

 - **Elijah Monday**: Eccentric orc parasol designer. First is fashion, second is utility.

 - **Dorothy 'Ghost Stuart**: The albino elf who maintains the Vague Cello. She knows everyone in town, but sometimes needs her wheels greased.

*Get your wares, get your goods, and survive the heat*
