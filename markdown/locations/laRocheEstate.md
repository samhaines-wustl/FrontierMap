# Madame LaRoche's Estate

*A fenced in swath of land surrounding a manor that wears the style of hundreds of years past*

### People
 - **Madame LaRoche**: An elderly woman obssessed with the past: antiquities, curios, and momentos. Even though she's enamored about the pearl standard, she makes sure to compensate you with other relics and items to reward you for accomodating her wishes.

 - **The Staff**: A collection of butlers and maids, all wearing pristinely pressed and tailored silk suits and dresses. They all have the same tick: they cannot move their head on their own, always needing to use their hands to pivot it.

*She acts ancient, but she doesn't look a day over 30*
