# Hope's Scar

*A superstitous town bordering cliffs*

### Places
 - **Poison Spring Cemetary**: Cementary outside Hope's Scar that has been passed down through the generations. The groundskeeper is paranoided about a giant skeleton awakening during the Bad Moon.
 
 - **Cliffside Caves**: Down where the waves crash into the cliffside are damp caverns home to the local sea witch

### People
 - **Ambassador of the Long Goodbye**: An upbeat marketer who has drawn the irritation of the town with their obnoxious booth. They spin tails of fortune and luck to draw people to the Long Goodbye.

 - **Marshal Berger**: The local marshal who is just trying to keep the peace during the Bad Moon. She won't tolerate any bad luck or omens during this trying time.

 - **Tillie Murphy the Sea Witch**: The target of the townfolk's spite. She manifests the Bad Moon and accepts sacrifices all in a misunderstood attempt to return her husband to her.

 - **Wallace Cain**: The humble groundskeeper for the cemetary. He doesn't have much time to talk since he needs to dig the new grave for the giant skeleton so it doesn't awaken.

 - **Surgeon Maddox and Casey**: Local represetatives of the Children of the Wax, an offshot of the Communion of the Lantern King. They will heal and aid anyone in need, no questions asked.
